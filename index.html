FFIEC Call Report Analytics Dashboard
Who it's for
Target users & problem solved
Bank examiners & regulators — currently download raw SDF zip files quarterly, run manual spreadsheet calculations, and wait days for peer comparisons. This makes it instant.
Financial analysts at banks — building UBPR ratios by hand from schedule line items. The formula builder exposes every numerator/denominator (UBPR code → field name) and computes across all peers in one query.
Research teams & academics — need longitudinal call report data without paying Bloomberg or navigating FFIEC's raw bulk downloads. 8 quarters, any bank, sub-200ms.
The core problem: FFIEC data is public but practically inaccessible — raw files, no query interface, no comparisons, no trend analysis. This platform closes that gap entirely.
Roadmap
Pipeline enhancements in progress
Financial Analysis module — side-by-side profitability, liquidity, and capital ratio comparison across up to 4 banks with ranked peer percentiles.
Statistical Analysis — distribution charts, z-score outlier detection, and correlation matrices across UBPR metrics for a selected peer group.
Predictive Statistics — time-series forecasting on key ratios (NIM, ROA, Tier 1 capital) using historical call report trends.
Prescriptions engine — AI-generated recommendations flagging ratios outside peer norms with suggested corrective actions grounded in regulatory guidance.
Google Scholar integration — inline citations linking ratio anomalies to published banking research papers.
User accounts & profiles — saved bank watchlists, custom ratio dashboards, and persistent query history per user.
Regulatory data platform that makes 8 quarters of FFIEC Call Report data queryable in under 200ms — down from 8–15 seconds via the live API. Bank examiners, analysts, and researchers currently download raw SDF zip files, calculate ratios manually, and have no way to compare peers dynamically. This platform eliminates all three friction points.

The UBPR formula builder lets users construct any ratio from raw schedule fields. Each formula shows the exact numerator and denominator with UBPR codes:

Academic · Machine Learning · HIPAA
Lackey Free Clinic — Patient Churn Prediction
End-to-end ML pipeline on HIPAA-restricted clinical and claims data for Lackey Free Clinic, a non-profit serving underinsured patients in Williamsburg, VA. The goal: predict which patients are at risk of disengaging so the clinic can do proactive outreach before they churn.

Data cleaning (Python): ICD-9 to ICD-10 mapping via patched lookup + CMS Section 111 reference achieving under 2% unknown. Charlson Comorbidity Index (17 conditions) and Elixhauser Index (31 conditions) computed per patient. Duplicate encounters resolved, date fields normalized.

Feature engineering: Recency, frequency, intensity, comorbidity score, SDOH proxies, specialty mix, visit trend direction. Churn label: no encounter in next 365 days.

Modeling (R, caret): 7 models with 5-fold CV — logistic regression, LDA, random forest, XGBoost, SVM, neural net, elastic net. OB/GYN specialty submodels trained separately after EDA showed distinct churn patterns. Final model selected on AUC at 0.3 threshold.
